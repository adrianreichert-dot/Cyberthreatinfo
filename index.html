HISTORIC CYBER THREATS

Explore eight infamous malware families and outbreaks that caused major disruptions around the world. Not all of them are technically computer viruses: some are worms, ransomware, or other forms of malware.

💌

ILOVEYOU

Discovered: 2000

The ILOVEYOU worm spread through email messages containing an attachment disguised as a love letter. Once executed, it spread to contacts and overwrote certain types of files.

Malware Type Email Worm
Year 2000
Primary Method Email Attachment
Historical Impact 95%
🔒

WannaCry

Discovered: 2017

WannaCry was a ransomware worm that spread rapidly across vulnerable Windows computers. It encrypted files and demanded payment to restore access.

Malware Type Ransomware Worm
Year 2017
Primary Method SMB Vulnerability
Historical Impact 98%
📧

Mydoom

Discovered: 2004

Mydoom became one of the fastest-spreading email worms of its era. It generated enormous amounts of email traffic and was associated with distributed denial-of-service attacks.

Malware Type Email Worm
Year 2004
Primary Method Email Propagation
Historical Impact 97%
🌐

Conficker

Discovered: 2008

Conficker infected millions of Windows computers and created a large botnet. It exploited Windows vulnerabilities and used multiple techniques to maintain infections.

Malware Type Network Worm
Year 2008
Primary Method Network Exploitation
Historical Impact 93%
⚙️

Stuxnet

Discovered: 2010

Stuxnet was an advanced computer worm designed to target industrial control systems. It became famous for demonstrating that malware could cause physical damage to industrial equipment.

Malware Type Industrial Worm
Year 2010
Primary Target Industrial Systems
Historical Impact 99%
💥

NotPetya

Discovered: 2017

NotPetya appeared to be ransomware, but its destructive design made recovery extremely difficult. The outbreak disrupted major international companies and caused billions in losses.

Malware Type Destructive Malware
Year 2017
Primary Impact Data Destruction
Historical Impact 100%
📄

Melissa

Discovered: 1999

Melissa was a macro virus that spread through infected Microsoft Word documents. It automatically emailed infected documents to contacts using Microsoft Outlook.

Malware Type Macro Virus
Year 1999
Primary Method Word Documents
Historical Impact 85%
🔐

CryptoLocker

Discovered: 2013

CryptoLocker became one of the most notorious early ransomware campaigns. It encrypted victims' files and demanded payment for decryption.

Malware Type Ransomware
Year 2013
Primary Impact File Encryption
Historical Impact 92%

ILOVEYOU

Mydoom

Conficker

HOW TO STAY PROTECTED

Understanding historical malware can help us defend against modern cybersecurity threats.

01. Update Software

Keep operating systems, applications, and browsers updated with the latest security patches.

02. Use Security Software

Enable reputable antivirus and endpoint protection tools to help detect malicious activity.

03. Avoid Suspicious Files

Be cautious with unexpected attachments, downloads, and links from unknown senders.

04. Back Up Important Data

Maintain regular backups, including offline or otherwise isolated copies, to reduce ransomware damage.

05. Use Strong Authentication

Protect accounts with unique passwords and multifactor authentication whenever possible.

06. Learn Cybersecurity

Study how malware spreads, how vulnerabilities are patched, and how security professionals investigate suspicious behavior.
